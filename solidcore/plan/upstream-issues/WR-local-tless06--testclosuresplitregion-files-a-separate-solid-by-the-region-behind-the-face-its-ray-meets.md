#### WR-local-tless06. `TestClosureSplitRegion` files a separate solid by the region behind the face its ray meets

- **Severity:** Wrong refusal (a valid union fails its check)
- **Area:** Region nesting, `topology.brep_p6`
- **Location:** the shell nesting at the end of `SmBrep::TestClosureSplitRegion` in `source/SMLib/src/SmBrep.cpp` ("Now sPnt = point on the current shell"), and the same reading of the ray fire's region for the face uses and wires it distributes: each other shell of the split region is given the region `pInnerShell->RayFire` returns, the region on the near side of the first face of the inner shell the ray meets.
- **What happens:** a split divides one region in two, the side the inner shell encloses and the rest. A point of another shell is on the inner side only when the ray meets the inner shell from inside, where the face's near side is the inner shell's own region. Met from outside, the near side of the face is whatever region lies behind it, and that is one of the split's two regions only while every face of the inner shell has one of them there. A union's tool standing on the body has its bottom face backed by the body's solid region: a separate solid of the same B-rep whose ray runs up through the body meets that face first, and its outside shell is moved into the body's solid region as an inner shell outside the region's outer shell (`SmBrep::AssertValid` test #12, "NonInfiniteRegion Inner shell is not geometrically inside its containing outerShell").
- **Trigger:** a 40 x 40 x 10 box with a 4 mm cube 2 mm below it (joined: one B-rep, two solids), united with a 25 x 25 x 10 box standing on the top face over where the (1, 1, 1) ray from the cube comes out. Found on T-LESS 06 rebuilt by SolidCraft's Reconstruct: four studs joined below the part (in a recess whose floor was not read), then a prism on its top; T-LESS 12 the same.
- **How found:** SolidCraft's scan rebuild of the T-LESS CAD models (2026-11-20): every join after the studs failed with test #12; with the studs left out, or joined last, it passed. A trace of the region lists through the merge showed the stud's outside shell leave the infinite region in `make_other_faces`, and a trace in `nest_shells` showed the ray from the stud meet the tool's shell at a face whose near side was the body's solid region.
- **Confirmed:** on the port, which follows the C++ here (read in `nest_shells` and `SplitShells::move_shell`); not run against the C++.
- **Rust port:** unconditional. `nest_shells` gives a shell the inner shell's region only when the ray met the inner shell from that region's side, and the other shell's region otherwise (no hit, as before, is the other's); `SplitShells::move_shell`, which places the face uses and wires, reads the ray the same way. Where every face of the inner shell is backed by one of the split's two regions this is upstream's answer. Test `a_union_on_a_body_with_a_separate_solid_keeps_that_solid_outside` (`crates/solidcore/tests/issue_388_island_in_void.rs`).
